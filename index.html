Meet Crisanchai Crisostomo: A Web Specialist building intuitive digital platforms and empowering the next generation of women in tech.

Explore Her Journey

The Spark of Curiosity

Crisanchai's journey into technology didn't start with code; it started with a question. Driven by a deep curiosity about how technology would shape our future world, she began looking for ways to make an impact.

Her passion fully ignited during her final year of college when she immersed herself in the EXPLICIT organization. Surrounded by an inclusive environment that championed equal opportunities, she was encouraged by peers and professors to step outside her comfort zone, leading her straight into the fast-paced world of hackathons and tech competitions.

Her Journey & Advocacy

Stepping Up & Speaking Out

Beyond the screen, Crisanchai took her advocacy to the stage, placing as 1st Runner-Up in the Mr. and Ms. PUP pageant. Her platform? Women's empowerment. She proved that women have the potential to lead and innovate in spaces traditionally seen as male-dominated.

The Hackathon Hustle

Teaming up with fellow students, Crisanchai consistently competed and placed in hackathons outside her campus. These high-pressure environments tested her technical skills and forged her ability to collaborate effectively under tight deadlines.

Leading with Empathy

During a major hackathon, Crisanchai was awarded Best Presenter. Her winning edge was her ability to answer the judges' questions with deep empathy, clearly bridging the gap between raw code and how the application would genuinely help its end users.

Navigating the Professional Leap

Transitioning from a student to a full-time professional brought new tools and shifting responsibilities. Crisanchai overcame this hurdle by leaning on the core skills she built in college: staying fiercely curious, asking questions, and relying on strong teamwork.

Milestone & Wins

Won 1st place at the 2025 FEU Create & Conquer Hackathon by co-developing "GabAI," a smart digital financial co-pilot.

1st Runner Up - GDG Sparkfest

Secured a podium finish at the 2025 GDG Sparkfest with "AgapAI," an AI-powered health protection and disease detection platform.

Award-Winning Presenter

Recognized as "Best Presenter" at the FEU Hackathon, showcasing brilliance and empathy in communicating tech solutions.

1st Runner-Up - Mr. & Ms. PUP

Successfully advocated for women's empowerment in technology on a university-wide stage.

Building Better Together

For Crisanchai Crisostomo, the most rewarding part of the day-to-day grind is the satisfaction of a completed feature. Every finished website is a small win that creates a more accessible digital world. She credits much of this success to diverse perspectives. In her current role, she collaborates closely with a female QA specialist whose meticulous attention to detail and user-centric perspective ensures their websites are as intuitive as possible.

Having someone to guide you is one of the most valuable things we can have. Mentorship plays an important role in growth and learning. As more women participate in competitions, projects, and leadership roles, they help inspire and empower the next generation of students to pursue careers in tech. — Crisanchai Crisostomo
